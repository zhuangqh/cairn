# Sample possession photos

`Cairn/Resources/SampleData/` holds the photos used by the DEBUG sample data
(`PreviewSampleData+Possessions.swift`) for previews and `-CairnSampleData`
screenshots. They are excluded from Release builds in `project.yml`.

Every image is CC0 or Public Domain Mark, found through
[Openverse](https://openverse.org). Subjects were cut out with
`PossessionPhotoService`, downscaled to 384 px, and quantized to 256-color
PNGs.
